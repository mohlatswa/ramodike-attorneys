How we can help you

Whatever your legal matter, we listen first, explain your options in plain language, and fight for the best possible outcome.

Divorce & Maintenance

Contested and uncontested divorces, child maintenance, custody and parental rights, handled with care and discretion.

Unlawful Arrest

Commercial & Corporate

Company matters, shareholder agreements, compliance and day-to-day legal support for businesses.

Contract Law

Drafting, reviewing and enforcing contracts so that your agreements protect your interests.

Deceased Estates

Administration of deceased estates, wills, executorships and the Master of the High Court process.

Road Accident Fund

Criminal Law & Bail

Urgent bail applications and criminal defence. Call us as soon as you or a loved one is arrested.

Labour & Employment

Unfair dismissals, CCMA and Labour Court matters, disciplinary hearings and employment contracts.

Managing Director · Attorney

Angy Dimpho Ramodike founded Ramodike Attorneys Inc. to give individuals, families and businesses access to legal representation that is approachable, transparent and effective.

With an LLB and an LLM from the University of Limpopo, she leads the firm's work across family law, criminal defence, civil claims against the State, RAF claims, labour disputes and commercial matters.
